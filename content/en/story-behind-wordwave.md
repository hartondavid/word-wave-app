Have you ever wondered how a simple idea turns into a fully functioning multiplayer game? WordWave started as a weekend project. Our goal was simple: create a fast, accessible word guessing game that anyone could play from their browser without needing to download an app.

We noticed that while there were many word games out there, few focused on real-time, synchronous multiplayer where everyone guesses based on the exact same definition at the same time. The thrill of typing the correct word just milliseconds before your friend is what we wanted to capture.

Building the first prototype was straightforward, but handling real-time state with multiple players required a robust backend. We went through several iterations, initially testing with local networks before moving to a cloud-based solution. Today, WordWave supports seamless multiplayer action, bringing friends and families together, no matter where they are.

This is just the beginning. We're constantly listening to feedback and planning new features to make the game even more exciting!
