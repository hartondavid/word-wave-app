WordWave is a game of speed and mental agility. If you find yourself consistently beaten to the punch by your friends, it's time to refine your strategy. Here are five tips to help you dominate your next session:

**1. Don't Overthink the Definition**
Often, the simplest answer is the correct one. If the definition is "A piece of furniture to sit on", don't start thinking about "chesterfield" or "ottoman" before you try "chair".

**2. Watch the Length Indicator**
The blank spaces are your biggest clue. If the word has 5 letters, you can immediately discard longer synonyms. Use this constraint to narrow down your mental search quickly.

**3. Type as You Think**
Don't wait until you're 100% sure. Start typing the prefix of the word you suspect it might be. The physical act of typing can sometimes jog your memory, and if it's wrong, you can quickly delete it.

**4. Practice Solo**
Use the Practice Mode! It's there for a reason. It helps you get familiar with the categories and the style of the definitions without the pressure of a live opponent.

**5. Stay Calm**
Panic is your worst enemy. If you miss a word, take a deep breath before the next round. The game is won over multiple rounds, not just a single fast guess.
