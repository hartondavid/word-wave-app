We've heard your feedback loud and clear: "I want to get better, but I keep losing to my friends!" Today, we're thrilled to announce the launch of **Practice Mode** in WordWave!

Practice Mode allows you to play the game solo. It uses the exact same dictionary and category logic as multiplayer, but without the pressure of an opponent typing faster than you. 

Here is what's new:
*   **Customizable Timers:** You can now choose between 30-second or 60-second rounds to test your speed.
*   **Letter Hints:** If you get stuck, you can enable gradual letter hints. The game will slowly reveal letters over time, helping you learn new words without immense frustration.
*   **Offline Support:** While you still need an internet connection to load the site, Practice Mode does not require a WebSocket connection, making it much faster and more reliable on spotty networks.

To access Practice Mode, simply hit the "Practice Solo" button on the homepage. We hope this new feature helps you sharpen your vocabulary and prepare for your next multiplayer showdown!
